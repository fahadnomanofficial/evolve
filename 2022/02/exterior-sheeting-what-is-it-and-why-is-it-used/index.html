Exterior sheeting: what is it and why is it used.

A closer look at exterior sheathing, where BoardeX is used, and why the wall system matters.

By Evolve1 February 2022
IN THIS STORY
What is exterior sheathing?Where it is usedMoisture resistance is not waterproofingConsider the whole wall

What is exterior sheathing?

BoardeX is an exterior façade board with a moisture-resistant core and an orange glass-fibre facing. It is used as part of exterior wall construction and as a base beneath other finishes.

Where it is used

The original article describes applications beneath metal, timber and decorative cladding, in ventilated façades, wet areas, shafts, soffits and beneath ceramic tiling. The choice depends on the complete wall assembly and the intended conditions.

Moisture resistance is not waterproofing

The article explicitly distinguishes BoardeX from a waterproofing material. Moisture resistance should not be treated as a substitute for a properly specified waterproofing system.

Consider the whole wall

Insulation, framing, cladding and board selection work together. Speak with your architect and the Evolve team about the appropriate system and current technical documentation for your project.

Adapted from Evolve’s original article, published 1 February 2022.
