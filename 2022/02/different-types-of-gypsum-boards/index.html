MATERIAL GUIDES

Different types of gypsum boards

A guide to the different roles gypsum boards can play in walls, ceilings and interior layouts.

By Evolve15 February 2022
IN THIS STORY
A flexible foundationChoose for the conditionsThe system mattersFinishing the surface

A flexible foundation

Gypsum boards are used for partitions, ceilings and interior details. They can help define rooms and create features such as recessed lighting.

Choose for the conditions

Standard white boards are commonly used indoors. Other boards are designed for increased fire resistance, moisture-prone areas or exterior sheathing. The original article also mentions specialist acoustic and impact-resistant options.

The system matters

A board’s suitability depends on its application and the complete construction. Mineral wool can be incorporated into wall and ceiling systems for thermal and acoustic requirements. Ask for a specification appropriate to your project.

Finishing the surface

A completed gypsum surface can be painted or finished with materials such as wallpaper and decorative panels. Prepare and prime the surface in line with the selected finishing system.

Adapted from Evolve’s original article, published 15 February 2022.
