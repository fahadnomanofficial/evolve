INTERIOR INSPIRATION

How to add life to your walls?

Give a room a focal point with colour, pattern, texture or a considered feature wall.

By Evolve29 July 2022
IN THIS STORY
A wall with personalityExplore pattern and textureBring in a green elementStart with colour

A wall with personality

The original article explores feature walls as a way to introduce a point of interest into your home. A single surface can change the character of a room.

Explore pattern and texture

Murals and wallpaper bring graphic detail. Marble cladding, brick panels and wood-plastic-composite finishes offer different ways to introduce texture and depth.

Bring in a green element

The article includes green walls among its ideas. Evolve’s product collection also includes artificial green walls; discuss which system suits the space and the level of maintenance you want.

Start with colour

Paint is another way to change the feel of a surface. Compare the options alongside your furniture, lighting and existing finishes, then speak with the showroom team about available products.
